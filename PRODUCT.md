# Internal Developer Platform Product Definition

## Product Name

Internal Developer Platform

## Mission

Enable engineering teams to build, deploy, and operate production-ready applications with minimum operational friction.

## Product Goals

- Reduce developer onboarding time
- Standardize application delivery
- Automate repetitive platform operations
- Improve developer experience
- Increase engineering velocity

## Target Users

### Developers

Need fast and reliable application delivery.

### Platform Engineers

Need scalable internal tooling.

### Engineering Managers

Need visibility and operational confidence.
