# Platform Principles

## Product First

The platform is designed around developer and engineering problems.

Technology choices must support measurable product outcomes.


## Self Service

Developers should complete common engineering workflows without manual platform intervention.


## Automation First

Repeated operational activities should become automated platform capabilities.


## Golden Paths

The platform provides recommended production-ready workflows.


## Ownership

Every service must have a clear owner and responsible team.


## Secure Defaults

Security controls should be integrated into platform workflows.


## Observability

Platform capabilities should provide visibility and operational feedback.
