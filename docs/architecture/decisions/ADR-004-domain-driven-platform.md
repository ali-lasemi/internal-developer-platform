# ADR-004 Domain Driven Platform Architecture

Status: Accepted


## Context

The platform manages multiple business capabilities including services, templates, teams, environments and deployments.

A structured domain model is required.


## Decision

The platform will follow domain-driven design principles.


Core domains:

- Service Management
- Template Management
- Workflow Management
- Environment Management
- Identity Management


## Benefits

- Clear boundaries
- Better maintainability
- Easier future expansion
- Reduced coupling
