# Customer Model

## Purpose

Define the users and customers of the Internal Developer Platform.

The platform is built around engineering teams as its primary users.


## Primary Customers

## Developers

Needs:

- Fast service creation
- Clear workflows
- Less operational complexity
- Better engineering experience


## Platform Engineers

Needs:

- Extensible platform capabilities
- Governance
- Automation
- Operational visibility


## Engineering Leaders

Needs:

- Productivity insights
- Delivery improvements
- Platform adoption visibility


## Customer Value

Developers gain speed.

Platform teams gain control.

Engineering leaders gain visibility.


## Success

A successful platform becomes the default path for software delivery.
