# Adoption Strategy

## Goal

Increase platform usage across engineering teams.


## Adoption Stages

Awareness

Teams understand platform capabilities.


Trial

Teams use selected workflows.


Adoption

Teams use platform as default workflow.


Expansion

Teams use advanced capabilities.


## Adoption Drivers

- Easy onboarding
- Valuable workflows
- Reliable platform
- Good documentation
- Clear benefits


## Adoption Metrics

- Active teams
- Active developers
- Created services
- Workflow usage
- User satisfaction
