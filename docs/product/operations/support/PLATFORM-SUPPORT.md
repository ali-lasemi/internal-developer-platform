# Platform Support Model

## Purpose

Define how platform users receive support.


## Support Levels

Level 1

Documentation and self-service guidance.


Level 2

Platform team assistance for workflows and capabilities.


Level 3

Engineering investigation for platform issues.


## Support Channels

Future capabilities:

- Documentation
- Issue tracking
- Notifications
- Internal communication channels


## Goals

- Fast resolution
- Clear ownership
- Reduced developer friction
