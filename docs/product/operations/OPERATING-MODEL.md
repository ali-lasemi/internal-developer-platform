# Platform Operating Model

## Purpose

Define how the Internal Developer Platform is operated, improved, and maintained.


## Operating Principles

Product Ownership

The platform is managed as a product with users, requirements, and measurable outcomes.


Continuous Improvement

Platform capabilities evolve based on feedback and metrics.


Reliability First

Platform availability and trust are critical.


Automation Driven

Manual operational processes should become automated workflows.


## Platform Team Responsibilities

- Maintain platform capabilities
- Manage technical standards
- Improve developer experience
- Monitor platform health
- Review platform metrics


## Development Process

Discover

Understand developer problems.


Design

Create product and technical solutions.


Build

Implement capabilities.


Measure

Analyze adoption and impact.


Improve

Iterate based on results.
