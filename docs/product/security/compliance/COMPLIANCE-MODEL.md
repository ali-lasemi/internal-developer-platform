# Compliance Model

## Purpose

Define compliance expectations for the Internal Developer Platform.

The goal is to maintain trust, transparency and controlled engineering practices.


## Compliance Areas

Access Management

Ensure users and services have appropriate permissions.


Data Protection

Protect sensitive information handled by platform capabilities.


Auditability

Maintain records of important platform actions.


Engineering Standards

Ensure services follow approved practices.


## Compliance Principles

- Clear ownership
- Traceable actions
- Documented decisions
- Automated validation


## Future Capabilities

- Compliance reporting
- Automated checks
- Policy dashboards
- Audit exports
