# Product Positioning

## Product Category

Internal Developer Platform

## Position

A self-service engineering platform that enables teams to build, deploy and operate software through automated workflows.


## Differentiators

Developer First

Designed around engineering workflows.


Product Approach

Built as a platform product, not only infrastructure.


Automation Driven

Reduces repetitive engineering work.


Extensible Architecture

Allows organizations to evolve capabilities over time.


## Value Proposition

For engineering teams that need faster and more reliable software delivery, Internal Developer Platform provides a unified self-service experience that reduces operational complexity.
