# Product Strategy

## Mission

Build the operating system for modern software engineering teams.

## Strategy

Create a self-service engineering platform that removes operational complexity from developers while providing platform teams with control, visibility, and automation.

## Strategic Pillars

### Developer Experience

Make software delivery simple and predictable.

### Automation

Replace repetitive engineering tasks with platform workflows.

### Standardization

Provide production-ready paths for teams.

### Engineering Intelligence

Provide visibility into delivery performance and platform adoption.


## Long Term Vision

An intelligent developer platform capable of managing the complete software lifecycle:

↓

Development

↓

Delivery

↓

Operations

↓

Optimization
