# Product Principles

## Developer First

Every capability must improve developer productivity and experience.


## Platform As Product

The platform is built with users, requirements and measurable outcomes.


## Automation By Default

Manual repetitive operations should become automated workflows.


## Opinionated But Flexible

Provide recommended paths while allowing evolution.


## Ownership Everywhere

Every service, workflow and capability has clear responsibility.


## Continuous Improvement

Platform decisions are based on feedback and metrics.
