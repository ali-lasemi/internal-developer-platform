# Ownership Model

## Principle

Every platform capability must have clear ownership.


## Product Ownership

Responsible for:

- Vision
- Requirements
- Priorities
- Success metrics


## Platform Engineering Ownership

Responsible for:

- Architecture
- Implementation
- Reliability
- Evolution


## Service Ownership

Every managed service requires:

- Responsible team
- Technical owner
- Documentation
- Lifecycle responsibility


## Decision Ownership

Architecture and product decisions must have:

- Owner
- Context
- Decision record
- Follow up actions
